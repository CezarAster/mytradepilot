How it works

From fills to verdict in four scrolls.

It reads your fills.

Any broker CSV with symbol, side, quantity, price, time. TradingView trade lists too. Fills are matched into round trips, duplicates dropped.

It tags every trade.

New York entry time, trade number of the day, what came before, hold time, direction, weekday, ticker.

It finds where the money is.

Your 1st trade of the day: 45 trades, 73% win, +0.94% a trade vs +0.33% overall. Solid.

And where it leaks.

Your 3rd trade of the day: 26 trades, 42% win, -0.81% a trade. Skipping them would have made your total +58.5% instead of +37.3%.

The report

Six questions the report answers.

Edges

Where you make money.

Groups that clearly beat your average, ranked by impact.

Leaks

Where you give it back.

Money-losing patterns first, then profitable-but-weak ones.

Stop

What your stop is doing.

Trades that went past your stop and came back vs. not, and your total if every one had been closed at the stop. Needs worst-point data (TradingView exports have it).

Concentration

How much rides on five trades.

In the sample, the 5 best trades made 65% of the total.

Breakdowns

When, how often, after what.

Entry time, trade number of the day, after a loss, quick re-entry, hold time, symbol, weekday.

Many files

Drop them all.

Overlapping exports are de-duplicated, partial fills merged.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

Is it really private?

Yes. Parsing and analysis run in your browser; the only network request the analyzer makes is for the sample file. The waitlist sends your email only.

What is "early signal"?

Fewer than 15 trades in the group. "Solid" is 15+. Under 5 is "too few" and never becomes an insight.

What does it cost?

The analyzer is free. The waitlist gets early access and founding-member pricing for the full app.
