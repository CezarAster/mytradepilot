Which files work?
  • TradingView: Bar Replay or Strategy Tester → List of trades → Export (CSV). Use your chart's time zone above.
  • Any broker: an executions/fills export with columns for symbol, side, quantity, price and time. Fills are matched into round-trip trades automatically.
  • Optional: a Worst % / Best % column on the closing fill (or TradingView's Adverse excursion %) turns on the stop check.

00 / Drop

One file. Any broker.

Drop a TradingView trade list or your broker's fills export. Several at once is fine: duplicates are dropped, partial fills merged. Nothing is uploaded; this tab does all the work.

01 / Fills become trades

232 fills become 112 trades.

Fills are paired into round trips, FIFO, the way your account actually moved. This LUMN position took six fills and was held overnight: it counts as one trade.

02 / The part every journal shows you

112 trades. 45 days. +$3,238.

61% winners, a 1.45 profit factor and a −$1,686 drawdown along the way. Useful. Not the point.

03 / Where you make money

Three edges, all solid.

Three groups beat this trader's +0.33% average after shrinking toward it, with enough trades to count: the first trade of the day, the 9:30–9:45 window, and longs.

04 / Where you give it back

The third trade of the day.

Third trades lost $2,346 across 26 trades. Skip them and +37.3% becomes +58.5%. Quick re-entries within ten minutes of a loss: −0.83% a trade, an early signal. Shorts: slightly under water.

06 / Honest by design

Five trades made 65% of the total.

Cut winners early and this whole report turns red. Groups under 5 trades are faded, not hidden. Small groups are shrunk toward the average, so three lucky trades are never called an edge.

Questions

Is it really free?
Yes. The analyzer is free, with no account and no limits on how many files you drop. The waitlist is for the full app, which will be paid; waitlist members get early access and founding-member pricing.
Where do my trades go?
Nowhere. The file is read and analyzed by code running in your browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and closing the tab erases the report. You can confirm it in your browser's network panel.
Is the sample real?
No. The sample is a fictional trader generated by a script in the repository (scripts/make-sample.py) with planted patterns, so you can see what the analyzer finds. Every number on this page is computed from that file by the engine, live.
